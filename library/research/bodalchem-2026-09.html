Why the squeeze is still structural

The mechanism is unchanged from August. H-acid runs on sulfonation and nitration, among the most polluting processes in chemistry, with difficult waste-acid disposal under strict environmental and safety control standards. Trade reporting through 2026 describes non-compliant small and mid-sized Chinese capacity being cleared continuously, and characterises industry supply as highly concentrated with very low elasticity. Permitting, not plant, is the binding constraint, which is why capacity does not return quickly.

The most striking single claim in the September reporting: China, historically a large net exporter of H-acid, is expected to become a net importer in September 2026. If that holds, it is a genuine structural inversion of the trade flow that suppressed Indian realisations for years. It is also, as of today, a forecast rather than a customs statistic.

The related intermediates confirm the pattern. A reduction intermediate used in disperse dyes moved from about CNY 25,000 per tonne at end-2025 to CNY 70,000–100,000 by March 2026 and had stabilised near CNY 100,000 by mid-year; the September trade press puts a disperse-dye intermediate at about CNY 120,000, roughly +380% from its end-2025 base.

The 4% Problem, Restated

Bodal’s Dye Intermediates segment declined 4% year on year in Q1 FY27, and operating margin compressed from 11.66% to 9.72%. This is the fact the rally is ignoring, and it deserves restating in full because it has not been superseded by anything.

Reported PAT figures circulate on standalone-versus-consolidated bases as ₹9.53 Cr, ₹28.78 Cr and ₹30.38 Cr. The +218.78% consolidated growth reconciles with the net-profit growth in the scan data and standalone revenue of ₹704 Cr reconciles with its +56.11% sales growth; both are used. The ₹9.53 Cr figure reconciles with neither and is not used. A consolidated screener view of the same quarter shows sales of ₹709 Cr and net profit of ₹30 Cr, consistent with the ₹30.38 Cr consolidated print.

Revenue grew 57% while EBITDA grew 43%. Growth came at a lower margin, not a higher one, and it came from elsewhere in the business — the Saykha benzene-downstream division began contributing to the topline in this quarter. Management attributed the result to strong volume growth, improved realisations across divisions, and the full pass-through of rising raw material costs. Full pass-through of input costs is a very different thing from capturing a supply squeeze: one defends the margin, the other expands it, and the margin fell.

Timing or positioning — the balance has shifted slightly

The August report set out two readings of the 4%, and said the timing reading was the more likely one because the dates supported it. The dollar series in Section 03 complicates that.

  • Timing. The decisive step change came on 28 July, after Q1 closed, and the near-doubling since is entirely a Q2 event. Q1 intermediates were still being sold into contracts struck in a depressed market. This remains the more likely explanation and the dates still support it.
  • Positioning. If Chinese H-acid was already up roughly a third across April–June on an independent quarterly assessment, a segment that shrank 4% in that window is harder to explain purely by lag. Bodal’s intermediates segment covers many molecules and H-acid is one of them; the segment may simply not be levered to H-acid the way the thesis assumes.

Q2 FY27 still distinguishes them, and nothing before then will. Intermediates swinging from −4% to strongly positive with margin expansion confirms the thesis. Intermediates staying soft says Bodal is not the right vehicle for it, whatever H-acid does — and at today’s multiple that answer would be considerably more expensive than it would have been in August.

Conflicting sources, both shown. One search result asserted that final findings on beta naphthol formed part of a March 2026 batch of DGTR notifications. The underlying newsletter that reference points to is dated 2025 and lists the entry only as an initiation — “Beta Naphthol from China PR — India initiates anti-dumping investigation”. We could not reach a primary DGTR page or notification confirming any preliminary finding, provisional duty, final finding or oral hearing date. On the evidence available, the correct statement is that the investigation is unresolved and the reported final-findings claim is unverified. It is not treated as fact anywhere in this report.

Two disciplines carry over unchanged from August, and both cut against reading this as an imminent catalyst:

  • An investigation is not a duty. DGTR proceedings run for many months, can conclude without a duty, and any recommendation still requires separate Finance Ministry notification before it has any commercial effect.
  • Being the applicant signals injury, not strength. A company files because dumping has been hurting it. The filing evidences a problem the company hopes to fix; the fix is not in place.

The precedent is real, though. In March 2025 the government imposed an anti-dumping duty on TCCA imports and Bodal was reported to have risen sharply on the notification. The market does price these outcomes when they actually land. That is an argument about the size of a future move, not about anything currently in the numbers.

Surveillance — ESM, ASM, and the 100% Margin

Bodal has been under exchange surveillance throughout this move, and the regime appears to have changed during it. The evidence is genuinely mixed and is set out here rather than resolved by assumption.

SourceAs atReads

Third-party surveillance dashboard, BSE view8 Sep 2026Long Term ASM — Stage I, entry 3 Sep 2026
BSE / NSE surveillance notice itself—Not retrievable — see Section 13

Last verified status and its date: ESM Stage I as at 28 August 2026, from the provider list in the scan. The Long Term ASM Stage I reading dated 3 September 2026 comes from a single third-party dashboard and could not be confirmed against an exchange notice; the relevant BSE and NSE documents returned HTTP errors or timeouts, and the circular archive we could reach indexes only to May 2026. It is stated here as an unconfirmed newer reading, not as an established fact.

A transition from ESM to ASM is plausible rather than contradictory. The two frameworks are alternatives, and one observable detail is consistent with the newer reading: the stock hit a 10% upper circuit on 7 September. ESM Stage II applies a 2% band under periodic call auction, and the tighter ASM stages apply a 5% band with trade-for-trade settlement. A 10% band is consistent with a stage whose only bite is margin. That is an inference from the price band, not a confirmation.

What each regime actually does

RegimeMarginTrading consequence
ESM Stage I100%Per an NSE circular, securities move to the trade-for-trade series
ESM Stage II100%Trade-for-trade with a 2% price band under periodic call auction
Long Term ASM Stage I100%Margin only at this stage; the price band is not tightened
Long Term ASM, higher stages100%Band reduced to 5%, trade-for-trade settlement, and an additional surveillance deposit at the higher stages

The practical consequence is identical under every one of them, and it should be stated plainly: ESM implies a 100% margin requirement, so there is effectively no MTF. Margin-funded exposure to this name is not available in any meaningful form, and ESM scrips are routinely excluded from broker MTF approved lists as a matter of policy independent of the margin rate.

Two further consequences follow. Escalation risk is an exit problem, not an entry problem — a move to a 5% or 2% band, or to trade-for-trade settlement, constrains getting out far more than getting in, and it arrives precisely when the price is moving fastest. And the flag is itself a signal: the exchange asked the company to explain the move, the company answered that there was nothing to explain, and the surveillance regime appears to have tightened days later.

Summary

RATING: HOLD AT CMP — THE RE-RATING ARRIVED BEFORE THE EARNINGS

The rating changes, and the reason is arithmetic rather than opinion. On 27 August this desk rated Bodal Accumulate on Dips at ₹98.67. That rating was not a call on the H-acid thesis, which was explicitly described as absent from every reported number; it was a call on the 1.07× book multiple that sat underneath the thesis and made it close to free. Twelve trading days later the price is up 86.5% (derived), the P/E has gone 18.12× → 33.80×, the book multiple 1.07× → 2.00×, and all three percentages are the same number because nothing in any denominator moved. The support that justified the buy rating has been consumed by the price.

The thesis itself is intact, unfalsified, and better evidenced than it was. Chinese H-acid spot is reported near CNY 100,000 per tonne against roughly CNY 40,000 at the start of 2026, with asks as high as CNY 150,000 — yuan, not rupees, which is the distinction that decides the magnitude. China is forecast to turn net importer of H-acid this month. The structural argument — sulfonation and nitration, waste-acid disposal, permitting rather than plant as the constraint — is unchanged and well supported. None of that is yet in a reported Bodal number.

And the timing gap is exactly where it was. Q1 FY27 ended on 30 June; H-acid stepped up on 28 July. Bodal’s Dye Intermediates segment — the segment that is supposed to benefit — fell 4% in that quarter while operating margin compressed from 11.66% to 9.72% and management described the result as a full pass-through of rising input costs. Q2 FY27, reported around November, is still the first quarter that can contain the thesis, and it is now the only thing that can. One new caution: an independent dollar assessment puts Chinese H-acid up roughly a third across April–June, which makes the 4% decline harder to attribute to lag alone.

Why Hold rather than Avoid, and why Hold rather than Accumulate. Not Avoid, because the framework is at maximum strength — RS 99 and a full seven-of-seven pass is precisely the configuration this system exists to identify, the trend is genuine, and the underlying commodity move is documented rather than rumoured. Not Accumulate, because the specific reason the August rating carried buy colour was an asset floor that no longer exists, and repeating a buy call at nearly double the price on an identical set of reported facts would be momentum reasoning wearing a valuation argument’s clothes. A rating is a statement about price against evidence. The evidence has not moved and the price has nearly doubled, so the rating moves the other way.

Held on a ₹2,322 crore name carrying an exchange surveillance flag that imposes a 100% margin requirement and therefore effectively rules out MTF, with the whole advance twelve days old and no base beneath it, this is now a position in an expectation that has already been paid for.
